A family workshop, still at work

Our family has worked with metal since 1750. What began as one small workshop has passed from each generation to the next, and the way we deal with customers has not changed. We explain what we have, quote a fair price and stand behind the metal we sell.

Many of our customers are the children and grandchildren of people who first bought from us.
